About

A lab built around research, not roadmaps.

Digital Wilderness runs sustained research programs across mathematics, electronics, avionics, and machine intelligence. The software and hardware we ship are downstream of that research — evidence it works, not the point of the exercise.

How we work

Three principles, held consistently.

Research leads.

Everything we ship traces back to a research question we were already trying to answer — not the other way around.

Constraints are honest.

Power, weight, latency, and budget are design inputs from day one, not obstacles to route around after the fact.

The unfinished work is shown too.

Our blog carries work in progress, not just finished results — a live feed of the lab, not a highlight reel.
